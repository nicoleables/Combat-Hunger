About Us

COMBAT HUNGER is a Texas 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization with one goal: to become a reliable and consistent source of high-quality beef protein for veterans receiving treatment for PTSD at Camp Hope in Houston through the PTSD Foundation of America.

Our Purpose

COMBAT HUNGER was formed out of a desire to give back and a commitment to serve those who have served our country. Our mission is to raise the funding necessary to purchase and provide high-quality processed beef from Texas producers to Camp Hope in Houston, supporting veterans receiving PTSD treatment through the PTSD Foundation of America.

We connect generous donors with Texas cattle producers to establish a reliable and consistent source of beef protein for veterans.

Our Focus

Our focus is on veterans receiving PTSD treatment and working toward healing at Camp Hope in Houston, Texas. We want to help provide a reliable and consistent source of high-quality beef protein to support their needs.

Future Goals

Our future goal is to establish the 52 Forever Fund, a permanent fund designed to operate in perpetuity and provide a stable, dependable source of funding for the beef needed by Camp Hope.

We want to build an enduring financial foundation so Camp Hope does not have to continually devote resources to finding protein. Our goal is not simply to feed veterans today, but to help ensure they are fed tomorrow and for generations to come.
